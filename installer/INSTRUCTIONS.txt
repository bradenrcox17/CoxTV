CoxTV for Roku - Windows installer
==================================

You need: a Windows 10 or 11 PC on the same Wi-Fi/network as your Roku.


STEP 1 - Turn on Developer Mode on your Roku (one time only)
------------------------------------------------------------
 1. On the Roku remote, press this sequence (nothing happens until the end):
        Home Home Home   Up Up   Right Left Right Left Right
 2. A "Developer Settings" screen appears. WRITE DOWN the IP address it shows
    (it looks like 192.168.1.50).
 3. Choose "Enable installer and restart" and agree to the license.
 4. Create a password and WRITE IT DOWN too. The Roku restarts.


STEP 2 - Install CoxTV
----------------------
 1. Double-click "Install CoxTV on Roku" in this folder.
    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
    then "Run anyway".
 2. Type your Roku's IP address and press Enter.
 3. Type the developer password from Step 1 and press Enter.
 4. Wait a few seconds for "SUCCESS". CoxTV opens on your TV.


STEP 3 - First time in CoxTV
----------------------------
 Select "Playlist (M3U) URL", type the playlist link from your TV service,
 then "Save and load channels". (Have an Xtream login instead? Choose
 "Enter Xtream login instead".)
 Tip: the free Roku app on your phone has a keyboard that makes typing long
 links much easier.


UPDATING
--------
 Run "Install CoxTV on Roku" again. It always installs the newest version and
 remembers your Roku's IP address and password: just press Enter twice.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
 "The Roku rejected the developer password"
     Run it again with the password from Step 1. Forgot it? Repeat the remote
     sequence in Step 1 to set a new one.
 "Could not reach a Roku"
     Check the IP address (Roku: Settings > Network > About), make sure the PC
     and Roku are on the same network, and that Developer Mode is on.
 Windows blocks the program ("Smart App Control blocked..." or no "Run anyway")
     Right-click the downloaded .zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then
     extract it again and use the new folder.
 "The installer's 'files' folder wasn't found"
     Extract the whole download first: right-click the .zip, "Extract All...".
 Keep the "files" folder next to "Install CoxTV on Roku" - it holds the
 installer itself.
